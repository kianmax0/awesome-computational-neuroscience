# Expansion evidence: methods, foundations, and research practice — 2026-10-22

## Scope and verification boundary

This screen fills gaps in biological background, likelihood-intractable inference, research design, acquisition context, and reproducible analysis. Official pages, documentation contents, examples, and institutional descriptions were inspected on 2026-10-22. No new package was installed or executed, no recording was acquired, no dataset or container was downloaded, and no course was attended. Companion tutorials and handbooks remain attached to their software or course rather than becoming additional catalog entries.
