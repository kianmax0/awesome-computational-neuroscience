# Curation research: computational psychiatry and normative modeling

Research date: 2026-10-22. The screen focuses on computational models of behavior and brain measurements in mental-health research, normative modeling, course material, and one open transdiagnostic dataset. The assigned scope excludes diagnosis or treatment recommendations. Existing HGF Toolbox, HSSM, and OpenNeuro archive entries were checked to avoid duplicate entries; the new dataset proposal is a specific OpenNeuro collection rather than a duplicate archive listing.

## Why these additions fit

Together, the five independent additions offer a sequence from conceptual framing and structured instruction to two distinct modeling routes: trial-level hierarchical models of decisions and regression-based normative models of brain measures. The normative-modeling protocol and hBayesDM article are companion links within the corresponding software entries, not separate catalog resources. The dataset provides real multivariate behavioral and MRI observations for an explicitly bounded research exercise. These methods answer different questions. Choice models describe a task's proposed latent computations; normative models estimate conditional reference distributions. A fitted parameter or deviation score is conditional on model specification, measurement, sample composition, and validation design.

The proposed guide therefore emphasizes parameter recovery, participant-grouped validation, site confounding, calibration, and the gap between research prediction and individual utility. The TCP release has 241 participants across two collection sites; that makes it a useful teaching dataset but a weak basis for unconstrained high-dimensional prediction or broad clinical generalization. Any interpretation must account for sample size, site-by-phenotype structure, task completion, missingness, and imaging quality.

## Verification limits

Official course, software, documentation, institutional publication, article, and dataset pages were opened on the research date. hBayesDM's documented language versions and PCNtoolkit's version boundary were checked directly. The normative protocol's data-selection, evaluation, and interpretation contents were inspected, but its code/API instructions predate PCNtoolkit 1.x and should not be treated as current executable guidance. The OpenNeuro snapshot metadata could not be rendered by the browser page itself; its indexed official dataset record and the primary dataset paper support the description and access conditions. No software was installed, models were fitted, or data were downloaded. The exercise remains an unrun proposal; links establish resource identity and documented scope, not scientific reproducibility or predictive validity.

## Final source correction

The final review updated the TCP dataset-paper companion from the 2024 unreviewed preprint to the [published 2025 Scientific Data descriptor](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-025-04895-z). The published descriptor and official dataset record support the stated population and two-site design. No data were downloaded.
