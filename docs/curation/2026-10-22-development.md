# Development and self-organization curation — 2026-10-22

## Scope

This review fills the documented gap for computational neural development with resources that directly support modeling neuronal growth, analyzing morphology, or constraining developmental models with data. Candidate pages, descriptions, abstracts, and documentation were inspected on 2026-10-22. No software was installed or executed, and no dataset was downloaded. For papers, publisher metadata and abstracts were checked; reading an abstract does not establish that the full article was reviewed. Access and software behavior may change.

The guide points readers to these project-maintained learning materials rather than adding a general neuroscience course. |

## Scope boundary

The selected set spans computational morphology, phenomenological growth simulation, developmental measurement, and a theory paper. It does not provide a comprehensive coverage of neurulation, cell lineage, molecular patterning, or activity-dependent refinement. The BrainSpan transcriptome is a constraint source rather than a simulator; mature morphology and gene-expression correlations cannot alone establish the causal sequence of circuit formation.
