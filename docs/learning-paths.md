# Learning paths and first projects

[Collection overview](../README.md) · [Current learning guide](../learn/README.md)

Learning routes now live in [learn/learning-paths.md](../learn/learning-paths.md). This page keeps earlier bookmarks usable. The routes and projects are editorial suggestions; the repository has not executed the exercises or established their results.

## Keep a short project record

Record the question, resources, inputs and settings, software environment where applicable, observable output, and limitation. Use [projects](../learn/projects.md) for bounded exercises and [reproducible research](../methods/reproducible-research.md) for a reusable research record.

## New to the field

Begin with [Start here](../START_HERE.md), choose a route in [learning paths](../learn/learning-paths.md), and use [prerequisites](../learn/prerequisites.md) to fill specific gaps.

## Modeling neurons and circuits

Use [neurons and dendrites](../topics/neurons-and-dendrites.md) or [circuits and dynamics](../topics/circuits-and-dynamics.md), then follow the [simulation workflow](../methods/simulation.md) and choose a small [project](../learn/projects.md).

## Working with recordings

Follow [neural data analysis](../methods/neural-data-analysis.md), starting with the recorded quantity and quality control, then use [fitting and comparison](../methods/fitting-and-model-comparison.md) if the question requires a model.

## Connecting brains and AI

Use the [NeuroAI topic guide](../topics/neuroai.md), a relevant [learning route](../learn/learning-paths.md), and an explicit [model-comparison procedure](../methods/fitting-and-model-comparison.md).
