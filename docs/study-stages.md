# Choose resources by experience

[Collection overview](../README.md) · [Current learning guide](../learn/README.md)

The current guides use [modular prerequisites](../learn/prerequisites.md), [learning routes](../learn/learning-paths.md), and [projects](../learn/projects.md). Mathematics, programming, biology, and experimental experience can differ within one person. The outcomes below remain optional ways to deepen a project; they are not certifications or a universal ranking of readers.

## 1. Build foundations

Aim to explain and modify one small model or describe one measurement. Use [Start here](../START_HERE.md) and the background bridge you need in [prerequisites](../learn/prerequisites.md).

## 2. Practice independently

Aim to complete a small analysis, derivation, or simulation whose choices you can explain. Choose a [project](../learn/projects.md) and a relevant [method](../methods/README.md); record inputs, units, and outputs.

## 3. Begin research

Aim to reproduce a bounded result and evaluate an alternative account. Connect a [topic question](../topics/README.md) to [theory and model building](../methods/theory-and-model-building.md), [fitting and comparison](../methods/fitting-and-model-comparison.md), and [reproducible research](../methods/reproducible-research.md) as appropriate.

## 4. Extend methods

Aim to specify a limitation, develop or adapt an approach, and test what the change achieves. State assumptions, suitable comparisons, uncertainty, failure cases, and the scope of the resulting claim. The [methods map](../methods/README.md) connects the relevant workflows.

## Keep the next project small

Choose one question, one main resource, one output, and one comparison. Revisit background material when a specific gap appears; a reader can enter any route at the point that fits their current task.
